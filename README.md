# Exploration of AI Use Case: Case Study on Self-Driving Cars

## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

## THEORY

Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence, sensors, cameras, and computer systems to travel with reduced or no direct human control, depending on their automation level.

AI plays an important role in identifying roads, vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signals, road signs, and obstacles. Technologies such as Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, and Path Planning help the vehicle understand its surroundings and select suitable actions.

Self-driving cars commonly use cameras, radar, and sometimes LiDAR to collect information about the environment. GPS and digital maps may help determine the vehicle's location and route. An onboard computer processes this information and controls steering, acceleration, and braking.

### Main AI technologies used

- Computer Vision: Identifies lanes, traffic lights, pedestrians, and other vehicles.
- Machine Learning: Helps the system recognize objects and patterns from data.
- Sensor Fusion: Combines information from different sensors to improve environmental awareness.
- Path Planning: Selects a suitable route and predicts safe movements.
- Decision-Making: Determines whether to stop, turn, slow down, or change lanes.
- Control Systems: Convert decisions into steering, acceleration, and braking commands.

## APPLICATION

One important application is AI-based autonomous driving in urban areas.

For example, when a self-driving car approaches a pedestrian crossing, its cameras and other sensors detect the pedestrian. The AI system estimates the pedestrian's movement, evaluates the situation, and decides whether to slow down or stop. The vehicle then applies the brakes and waits until it is safe to proceed.

Other applications include:

1. Autonomous taxis and ride-sharing services.
2. Driver-assistance systems, such as automatic emergency braking and lane-keeping assistance.
3. Self-driving delivery vehicles.
4. Autonomous trucks for goods transportation.
5. Parking assistance and automated parking systems.
6. Traffic sign recognition and collision avoidance.

##  WORKING PROCEDURE



Step 1: Data Collection

Cameras, radar, LiDAR (if equipped), GPS and other sensors collect information about the road, nearby vehicles, pedestrians and obstacles.


Step 2: Object Detection

Computer vision identifies lanes, traffic lights, road signs, pedestrians and nearby vehicles from camera images.


Step 3: Environment Understanding

Sensor fusion combines available sensor data to estimate the positions and movements of objects around the vehicle.


Step 4: Route Planning

The navigation system determines the route to the destination and selects a safe path based on road conditions and nearby traffic.

Step 5: Decision-Making

The AI evaluates the situation and decides whether to continue, slow down, stop, turn or change lanes.


Step 6: Vehicle Control

The control system sends commands to the steering, accelerator and braking systems to carry out the selected action.


Step 7: Continuous Monitoring

The sensors continuously collect new data, and the AI repeats the process to respond to changing road and traffic conditions.

## RESULT

Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied. The roles of computer vision, machine learning, sensor fusion, route planning and vehicle control were understood. These technologies help autonomous vehicles detect obstacles, make driving decisions and navigate roads. However, safe operation depends on the vehicle's capabilities, operating conditions, testing and appropriate human supervision where required.
